Definitions: active buyer = at least one purchase in trailing 12 months; active seller = at least one charge in trailing 12 months.

**Habitual buyers** (at least $200 spend and 6 purchase days in trailing 12 months): 5.9M at end-2025, -9% YoY; ~7% of active buyers but ~40% of 2025 GMS. Peak ~8.1M at end-2021 [K]. In Q2 2026 habitual and repeat cohorts each showed slight sequential gains for the first time since 2023.

**GMS per active buyer (TTM):** $122 at Q1 2026; $124 at Q2 2026 (+2.8% YoY). Gross buyer additions +7.1% YoY in Q2 2026. Implied FY2025: ~$10.5B / 86.5M = ~$121.

**Repeat-purchase rate:** historically ~50% of active buyers were repeat buyers [K, 2021 to 2023 10-Ks]. No 2025 percentage surfaced.

## 3. Traffic, Mobile, Geography

- Similarweb: etsy.com ~462.1M visits/month (July 2026); global rank #67; #5 in Marketplace category. [S] https://www.similarweb.com/website/etsy.com/ via https://bloggingwizard.com/etsy-statistics/
- Late-2025 run-rate ~450M visits/month, peaking over 500M in December. [S] https://thunderbit.com/blog/etsy-stats
- Device split: 64.49% mobile / 35.51% desktop. [S] https://ecommercebonsai.com/etsy-statistics/
- App alone ~46% of GMS in Q4 2025 and ~47% in Q1 2026; app GMS +11.2% YoY in Q1 2026. [S] https://www.printful.com/blog/etsy-statistics ; TradingView
- US ~74% of buyer GMS. [S] https://linkmybooks.com/blog/how-many-sellers-are-on-etsy . Etsy's own "international GMS" metric (buyer OR seller outside US) was ~45% to 47% in 2022 to 2024 [K].
- Traffic share by country: UK 9.25%, Canada 5.05%, Germany 4.3%, France 2.99%. [S] Link My Books. Core geographies: US, UK, Germany, Canada, Australia, France [K].
- 28% of sellers export internationally. [S] Link My Books
- Agentic-AI referrals: 15x YoY growth in Q4 2025 but under 1% of traffic. [S] https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/etsy-three-way-ai-bet-starting-pay-off/
