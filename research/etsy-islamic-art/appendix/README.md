# Appendix: raw research threads

Five parallel research threads produced the evidence consolidated in the main report. Each file below is the unedited output of one thread, including its full source list and its own statement of what could not be verified. Figures in the main report were cross-checked between threads where they overlapped (for example, Etsy fee arithmetic appears independently in threads 3 and the model, and Q2 2026 Etsy results appear in threads 1 and 4).

| File | Thread | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| `01_etsy_marketplace_fundamentals.md` | 1 | GMS, buyers, sellers, traffic, fees, policy, competitors, corporate strategy 2015 to 2026 |
| `02_islamic_art_niche.md` | 2 | Shops, sales counts, prices, demand signals, seasonality in the Islamic art niche |
| `03_seller_economics.md` | 3 | Seller income distribution, POD and metal unit costs, conversion benchmarks, fee stacks, IP and religious-respect constraints |
| `04_seller_scaling_history.md` | 4 | Case studies of scaled sellers, Muslim-market brands, shop lifecycle, marketplace timeline, sales correlations |
| `05_channels_and_muslim_market.md` | 5 | Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Etsy tools, wholesale, events, influencer networks; Muslim population, spending, art market |

Environment note common to all threads: etsy.com, SEC EDGAR, Wayback Machine and most data-tool domains were blocked by the sandbox network policy, so Etsy figures come from search-engine snippets of the cited pages rather than live reads. Each thread flags its own gaps.
