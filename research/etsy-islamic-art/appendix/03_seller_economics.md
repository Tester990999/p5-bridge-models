# Thread 3: Unit Economics and Profit of Etsy Art / Wall-Decor / Print Sellers (September 2026)

Method note: every fetch target was egress-blocked; figures come from search-engine extracts of the cited pages. Etsy 10-K and press-release numbers are the only primary-source figures; third-party statistics blogs are indicative.

## 1. Distribution of Etsy seller income

**Platform-level (primary source)**
- 5.6M active sellers at 31 Dec 2025. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370637/000137063726000019/etsy-20251231.htm
- Etsy marketplace GMS 2025 $10,460.7M, so ~$1,868 GMS per active seller per year.
- Active buyers 86.5M (-3%); GMS per active buyer $121 TTM. https://investors.etsy.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/218/etsy-inc-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results
- Q1 2026 GMS per active buyer $122. https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/article/etsy-revenue-gms/
- Fewer than ~500k sellers generate over $10k/yr (~9% of 5.6M). https://www.skillademia.com/statistics/etsy-statistics/
- Top 1% of sellers ~30%+ of GMS (third-party estimate). Skillademia.
- ~1.7M shops have any lifetime sales. https://linkmybooks.com/blog/how-many-sellers-are-on-etsy ; https://www.marketplacepulse.com/top-etsy-sellers

**Etsy 2024 Global Seller Census**
- ~30% say Etsy is their sole occupation; 34% hold full-time employment elsewhere; 80% women; 80% businesses of one; 97% run from home; 91% sole proprietors. https://www.etsy.com/news/the-creative-entrepreneurs-behind-etsyas-special-items ; https://www.patreon.com/posts/125639678 ; https://www.statista.com/statistics/290521/self-identification-and-motivation-of-etsy-sellers/

**Income brackets (survey/third-party)**
- ~50% earn under $1,000/yr. https://printify.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-on-etsy/
- ~70% of shops earn under $100/month. https://www.craftpilot.io/blog/etsy-shop-profitability
- UK-framed: ~50% of active shops under £200/mo; ~25% £200 to £1,000; ~15% £1,000 to £5,000; ~10% over £5k/mo. https://www.insightagent.app/guides/average-etsy-seller-income
- Median seller revenue ~$574/month; mean ~$2,965/month. https://customcy.com/blog/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make/ ; https://blog.marmalead.com/how-much-can-you-make-on-etsy/
- 17% of shops over $2,000/month; 13% over 2,000 sales/yr. Customcy.
- Typical annual profit for an established seller $7,116 to $17,791 (20% to 50% margin on ~$35.6k revenue). Customcy.
- Top shops: lifetime revenue near $20M, annual over $2M. https://www.statista.com/statistics/1306498/top-etsy-sellers-sales-volume

**Digital-download income**
- Median digital seller £400 to £800/mo; top 10% £8k to £30k+/mo from 50 to 200 listings. https://www.insightagent.app/guides/selling-wall-art-digital-downloads-etsy
- Ramp: months 1 to 2 $0 to $100; months 3 to 6 $200 to $800; months 6 to 12 $800 to $2,500; year 2+ $2k to $10k+/mo. https://www.listifyai.net/blog/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make-2026
- Case: $93k revenue selling digital downloads. https://ammarosedesigns.com/how-to-i-made-93k-in-revenue-selling-digital-downloads-on-etsy/
- Outlier wall-art shop: 472k+ lifetime sales, ~8,600/month, 35,171 reviews. https://thecreativeshour.com/etsy-wall-art-stores/
