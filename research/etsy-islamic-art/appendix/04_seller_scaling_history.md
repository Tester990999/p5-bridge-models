## 3. Shop lifecycle

- 5.6M active sellers Dec 2025; over 10M registered shops including dormant. https://www.printful.com/blog/etsy-statistics ; https://www.ecommercebytes.com/2026/02/20/etsy-marketplace-shows-flat-growth-in-gms-in-4th-quarter-of-2025/
- Active sellers +5.9% to 5.7M in Q2 2026; ~87M buyers; GMS/buyer $124. https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/etsy-inc-etsy-q2-2026-231603652.html
- Seller decline attributed to "intentional impact of the seller set-up fee introduced in 2024". eCommerceBytes.
- Of 3,168,606 shops analysed, 829,920 (26.2%) had a sale in a 30-day window; median 30-day sales 0. https://www.insightagent.app/guides/average-etsy-seller-income
- Time to first sale: days to two months; 1 to 3 weeks depending on views; 3 to 6 months normal without an audience. https://tizzit.co/how-long-will-it-take-to-get-sales-from-your-handmade-or-etsy-shop/ ; https://techpenny.com/when-expect-first-sale-on-etsy/ ; https://multipassionateprofitclub.com/how-long-to-make-sales-on-etsy/ ; https://www.meersworld.net/2026/02/how-long-does-it-take-to-get-sales-on-etsy-realistic-timelines.html ; https://sarahjwaggoner.com/how-long-does-it-really-take-to-make-your-first-sale-on-etsy/
- Listings vs sales (Listadum, 5M+ shops): no strong correlation; top 1% median 183 listings; most 20k to 50k-sale shops under 300. https://www.listadum.com/blog/how-many-listings-should-i-have-on-etsy
- 5+ reviews: 270% more likely to be purchased; 57% require 4+ stars. https://wisernotify.com/blog/social-proof-statistics/
- Purchase probability peaks 4.0 to 4.7 stars. https://genesysgrowth.com/blog/social-proof-conversion-stats-for-marketing-leaders
- Conversion climbs to 2% to 4% after ~50 sales. https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-conversion-rate-benchmarks
- Star Seller up to 22% higher conversion (estimate); Etsy says no direct search effect but 2026 guidance notes ranking lift. https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/etsy-star-seller-implications-concerns ; https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403058372503-What-is-the-Star-Seller-Badge ; https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1099574425189
- Bestseller badge lifts CTR and visibility. https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/what-is-etsy-bestseller-badge ; https://nancybadillo.com/what-is-etsy-bestseller-badge/

## 6. Ramadan / Eid seasonality

- One owner: Eid orders drove a 20% sales increase and ~200 pre-orders. https://thestoryexchange.org/muslim-women-owned-businesses-see-strong-sales-ramadan-eid/
- "Record boost in sales" for Muslim decor businesses. NPR; https://amcob.org/blog/ramadan-marketing-tips-for-business
- "Ramadan clothing" interest peaked Dec 2025; "Ramadan home decor" rose early 2026. https://www.accio.com/business/2025-trending-islamic-products
- Ramadan-decorations interest index 100 in March 2025; MENA searches +50%; decor mentions +19%. https://www.brandwatch.com/blog/ramadan-consumer-insights/ ; https://www.accio.com/business/ramadan-trend-2025
- Spending mix (KSA): food 34%, clothing 19%, restaurants 15%; home decor among top categories after. https://themiddleeastinsider.com/2026/03/14/saudi-ramadan-economy-2026-spending-retail-trends/
- Luxury search +21% UAE, +45% KSA late Ramadan. Brandwatch.
- Hajj / Eid al-Adha secondary spike. Salaam Gateway.
- Retail lead times 6 to 12 months. Retail Insider; https://www.foryoudecor.com/insights/ramadan-2027-decor-sourcing-trends-compliance.html
- Calligraphy wall art is year-round; lanterns, banners, tableware are seasonal. Brands that scaled into mainstream retail lead with seasonal.
